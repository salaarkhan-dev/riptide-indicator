VERDICT: INCONCLUSIVE. Criterion 1 met, criterion 2 missed, guards clear, and
the run was adequately powered. Per PREREG §6 the LIT trading family is closed
permanently.

1. THE PIPELINE IS CONFIRMED.

   The reproduction check on the Stage A/B symbols returns T6_PIVOT at +0.145
   R/bet, t=2.5, on 3195 bets — Stage B's number to three decimals. Whatever
   Stage C says, it is not saying it because the code changed.

2. THE EFFECT DID NOT VANISH. IT SHRANK, AND MISSED THE BAR.

                             Stage A/B      untouched tier
    T6_PIVOT, standalone        +0.145           +0.114   (t 2.5 -> 2.4)
    paired delta vs control     +0.120           +0.068   (z 2.5 -> 1.5)

   Criterion 1 is met: the arm is positive at t=2.4. Criterion 2 is not: the
   paired delta is +0.068 at z=1.5 against a required 2.0.

   Criterion 2 is the one that matters and PREREG §6 required both for a
   reason. The standalone number cannot isolate the trail, because the
   BOS_TARGET control is also positive here (+0.047). Only the paired
   difference says whether trailing is doing anything.

3. THIS IS NOT AN UNDERPOWERED NULL, WHICH MAKES IT WORSE, NOT BETTER.

    achieved: 3027 bets, SE 0.0468, MDE at t=2.0 = 0.094R

   Stage B's paired delta of +0.120 would have registered at z=2.6 on this
   sample. The experiment could see the effect it was looking for and found
   roughly half of it. The §4 underpower declaration does not fire.

4. THE GUARDS ARE CLEAN, AND THAT IS WORTH RECORDING.

    mark-to-market   3.0% horizon exits, contributing +0.010R/trade
    risk unit        P(loss > 1.5R) = 1.3%, median loss 1.01R, worst 4.03R

   Neither of the two ways this measurement could have lied is what happened.
   The trail result, such as it is, comes from real stop exits on a real risk
   unit. Stage A's failure mode is fully absent here.

5. THE MOST PARSIMONIOUS READING, STATED PLAINLY.

   Shrinkage of roughly half on an out-of-sample tier is the signature of the
   winner's curse. T6_PIVOT was the best of 25 cells in Stage B; the best of
   25 cells regresses. A real-but-smaller effect produces the same picture and
   one test cannot separate the two. What can be said is that the honest
   estimate of the trail's advantage is no longer +0.120 — the pooled reading
   across both tiers is closer to +0.09, and at that size it is not
   distinguishable from zero on any sample this project can assemble.

6. NO RESCUE, AND NO STAGE D.

   PREREG §0 committed against a fourth pre-registration before any of these
   numbers existed, and §6 closes the family on INCONCLUSIVE. Both hold. The
   directionally-consistent result is recorded, not argued past: pooling the
   two tiers to manufacture significance, or re-testing on a narrower slice,
   is precisely the fitting-by-iteration this sequence was built to stop.
