The rules, when you have forgotten one

Entering
  • Limit at the alert's entry. Never market. Market-entering measured −0.064 R on confirmed and −0.048 on early. The limit order is doing the selecting.
  • Leave it 10 bars, then cancel. 5 hours on 30m, 2.5 on 15m. Cancelling at 5 bars costs 0.069 R per confirmed setup.
  • Never chase it. Moving the order toward price costs 0.066 R at half an ATR and 0.230 R at a full one — the stop does not move with the entry.
  • An unfilled setup is a zero, not a loss. 20–30% never fill and that is priced in.
Managing
  • Exit at 2R. The ladder is monotone through 3R; 2R is a floor, not a ceiling.
  • Never move the stop to break-even. Arming at 1R costs 0.074 R per confirmed setup and drops total R from +53 to +35. 26% of confirmed setups reach 3R.
  • Never widen the stop. Swept 0 to 1.0 ATR; zero buffer is the peak on both types.
  • Never cut it short. Shortening the horizon 60→20 bars is the worst exit result measured.
Choosing
  • A before B; between two B's, neither is better. A is +0.271 R per signal, B +0.180.
  • A wins 48%. More than half lose. It pays because of the 1.81:1, not because it is often right. Three losers in a row is an ordinary week.
  • The BTC line is a fact, not a verdict. Its labels were inverted by a bug for weeks. Corrected, it does not survive its robustness split. Ignore it in both directions.
  • Never size up on an A. The letter changes which trades you take, never how much you risk.
  • Never act on a sweep heads-up. No entry and no stop because neither exists yet.
