============================================================================================
  READ — T8 IS NOT RESOLVED BY THIS, AND WHY
============================================================================================
  The two definitions BRACKET the source's object without hitting
  it, and neither result should be quoted as the T8 answer.

  A carries a pivot from an earlier structure, so the target sits
    a median 7.8% of price away and loses the race by construction.
  B scopes it to the live cycle and finds NO qualifying grabs at
    all: at the moment of a BOS grab the opposite pullback has not
    formed yet. It forms during the reaction the grab causes.

  So the countertrend target is not a level that exists in
  lit_v3's event stream when the grab happens. The Pine engine
  maintains BOTH directions live via the P8 idle observer, which
  is where its 'BOS near -> Opp PB reach' figure comes from; the
  Python engine used here does not expose that.

  T8 THEREFORE REMAINS UNRESOLVED. Resolving it needs the
  dual-direction detector ported to Python first. No countertrend
  behaviour is shipped, and no rate is claimed.
