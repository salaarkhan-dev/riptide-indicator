the three ways to lose here
✕ Taking the 👀 WATCH alerts too. That is the 0.13 column.
✕ Holding more than ~5 positions at 1% risk. Ten open returned −39% over the same year.
✕ Waiting for a better entry. The edge decays 0.06 R every 15 minutes — ten times the whole per-trade edge.
