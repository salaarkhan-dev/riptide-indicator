VERDICT: INCONCLUSIVE. Criterion 2 passes overwhelmingly. Criterion 1 fails.
Per PREREG §5 the LIT trading family is closed.

1. THE STOP FIX WORKED COMPLETELY.

    realized loss      median   P(>1.25R)  P(>1.5R)   worst
    RAID (Stage A)      1.13R      28.5%     14.3%   22.45R
    PRIOR_PB (primary)  1.02R       4.3%      1.9%    9.45R

   Against a 10% ceiling, 1.9%. The risk unit is now real: a loss is a loss of
   about 1R, which is what 1R is supposed to mean. Stage A's diagnosis was
   correct and the remedy did exactly what it was predicted to do.

2. AND WITH A REAL RISK UNIT THE EDGE DISAPPEARS.

    control exit     RAID stop    PRIOR_PB stop
    FIXED_1R            -0.478           -0.028
    FIXED_2R            -0.296           +0.021
    BOS_TARGET          +0.806           +0.024

   BOS_TARGET falls from +0.806 R/bet to +0.024. The collapse is not a
   degradation, it is the artefact being removed: median distance to BOS falls
   from 8.67R to 1.25R over the same setups, because the denominator stopped
   being a single candle's wick. Stage A's +0.806 was 20% of trades paying
   ~8R of a fictional R. There was never 0.8R/bet there to lose.

3. THE MFE OPTIMISM IN STAGE A WAS THE SAME ARTEFACT, AND IS WITHDRAWN.

   Stage A reported that among setups reaching Active Price the median MFE was
   1.91R with 48.3% exceeding 2R, and called that "real favourable excursion".
   With an honest denominator:

    among Active-Price reached   median MFE   >2R
    RAID (Stage A)                    1.91R  48.3%
    PRIOR_PB (primary)                1.09R   8.1%

   The excursion was measured in the same fake units as everything else. The
   event does not produce large favourable moves relative to a real risk unit.
   That sentence in lit_stage_a_out.txt is hereby retracted.

4. THE SECONDARIES AGREE, INCLUDING THE ONE THAT WAS REQUESTED.

   IDM_PIVOT behaves exactly as PREREG §2 predicted from geometry alone: at
   0.347% median width it is NARROWER than RAID, its realized-loss tail is the
   worst of all five (P(>1.5R) = 19.9%), and its BOS_TARGET arm shows the same
   +0.737 lottery. It reproduces Stage A's failure rather than fixing it.

   LEG and CHOCH, both wider, land where PRIOR_PB lands: every control within
   0.03R of zero. Three independent stop widths spanning 2.9% to 12.2% of
   price all say the same thing, which is the strongest part of this result.

5. WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED, AND THE ONE THING STILL ALIVE.

   The trailing arms are the best cells on the primary stop — T6_PIVOT +0.145
   (t=2.5) and T6_STRUCTURE +0.138 (t=2.2), both beating BOS_TARGET on paired
   deltas (z = 2.5 and 2.4). PREREG §5 restricted criterion 1 to fully
   specified controls precisely because T6 is an open [GAP] in the source, so
   these cannot and do not deliver a PASS.

   They are recorded, not claimed. Across 5 stops x 5 exits = 25 cells, a
   |t| of 2.5 is close to what chance produces, and the two trail arms are not
   independent of each other. Pursuing them would require a new
   pre-registration with a trail as the primary endpoint — which is the
   fitting-by-iteration PREREG §5 forbids. That call belongs to a human, not
   to this process continuing on its own momentum.

CONCLUSION. The LIT structure engine is sound and externally validated
(ZEC 30m IDM->BOS 65.6% against the reference's 65.0%). The LIT trading
family, as specified across two pre-registered experiments and five stop
definitions, does not show an economic edge net of costs. Stage A failed on a
broken risk unit; Stage B fixed the risk unit and found roughly zero.
