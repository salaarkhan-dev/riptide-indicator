==============================================================================
  HOW TO READ THIS
==============================================================================
  1. t below 2 means R per trade is not distinguishable from zero,
     however good the total looks.
  2. 'TOTAL without best' and 'without top 5' are the honest
     version of total R. If they collapse, the total was a handful
     of trades and will not repeat.
  3. 'best month as % of total' is the same test in time. A
     strategy can be spread across trades and still be one month.
  4. Longest LOSS run and max drawdown are what you would actually
     have had to sit through.
  5. These are HISTORICAL bars the candidate was developed on.
     Stage C already showed the effect halves out of sample. Read
     this as a description of the past, not a forecast.

That is not a strategy with some luck in it. That is luck with a strategy
around it. And t = 1.80 says the same thing without any of the arithmetic.

Both BOS-target arms are near zero or negative, and their median trade is

beating a bad control is not evidence the trail is good, and the paired test in

A NOTE ON WHY THESE NUMBERS DIFFER FROM STAGE C

Stage C scored every setup and reported per BET. This report takes ONE POSITION
AT A TIME PER SYMBOL, chronologically, and reports per TRADE. That is the more
realistic execution and the less favourable statistic, because the skipped
overlaps were not random. Neither is wrong; they answer different questions.
The per-bet number is the one the pre-registration uses for inference.

WHAT THIS IS AND IS NOT

This is a description of 333 days of HISTORICAL bars the candidate was built
on. It is not a forecast. Stage C already showed the effect halves on symbols
the work never touched, and the settings grid showed per-pair tuning decays by
0.95 R out of sample.
