== 2. WHAT THE ORIGINAL STUDY COULD DETECT =================================
  MDE = 2.80 x sd x sqrt(2/n), sd = 1.31 per bet.
  the original cells and held-out arms, with the effect each
  could have resolved at 80% power:

  cell                                   n   reported      MDE
  discovery Min15 confd in POI          31+0.417±0.222    0.932
  discovery Min15 confd no POI          28     -0.177    0.980
  discovery Min30 confd POI+trend       43+0.822±0.187    0.791
  held out Min30 confirmed              30+0.429  +1.4SE    0.947
  held out Min15 early                 122+0.201  +1.8SE    0.470
  held out Min30 early                 170+0.066  +1.3SE    0.398

  every one of those needed an effect of 0.4 to 0.9 R to reach
  2 SE. the largest effect this project has measured anywhere,
  on any question, is +0.32.
