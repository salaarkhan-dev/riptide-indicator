The stop specified for Stage A - the IDM raid extreme with no buffer - is the
break bar's own wick, measured from that same bar's close. A quarter of setups
have a stop tighter than 0.18% of price. That is inside ordinary crypto noise,
and the consequences run through every number above:

  - The risk unit is fictional. 99% of losses exceed the planned 1R, the
    median loss is 1.12R and the worst is 22.45R. You cannot be filled at a
    stop that tight, so "1R" never happens. Position sizing off it would be
    wrong in the unsafe direction.
  - The median trade is dead in one bar.
  - BOS at a median 8.67R away is not a rich target, it is a tiny denominator.
    BOS_TARGET wins 20% of the time at roughly 8R, which arithmetically gives
    +0.8R/bet. It is a lottery ticket, not an edge, and its SE of 0.256 on
    3632 bets says the same thing.
  - Fees scale inversely with the stop: 0.076R at the median stop but 0.418R
    at the p10 stop, on the repo's own 0.032% round trip.

That last line also explains why earlier LIT work concluded "no stop distance
works at all". At the p10 stop the old 0.10% fee assumption cost 1.31R per
trade - more than the entire risk unit. The fee error was not a uniform
handicap; it was catastrophic precisely because this stop is degenerate.

WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT SAY.

  It says the stop as specified is not viable. The failure is located in the
  stop definition, not demonstrated in the entry event.

  It does NOT say a wider or structural stop would work. That has not been
  measured, and measuring it now - after seeing these numbers - would be
  exactly the post-hoc fitting PREREG §10 forbids. Any such test needs its own
  pre-registration.

  The trails are consistent with the rest: T6_PIVOT and T6_STRUCTURE both lose
  to BOS_TARGET on paired deltas (z = -1.4 and -0.2), so neither rescues
  anything, and T6 remains unanswered rather than answered positively.

  The MFE diagnostic is the one genuinely encouraging reading: among the 60.0%
  of setups reaching Active Price, median MFE is 1.91R and 48.3% exceed 2R.
  There is real favourable excursion in the event. It is the risk unit
  underneath it that is broken.

NEXT STEP, NOT TAKEN HERE: a pre-registered test of a structurally-defined
stop (the IDM pivot rather than the break bar's wick), which would give a risk
unit that survives contact with the market. Until that is registered and run,
no LIT trading claim stands.
